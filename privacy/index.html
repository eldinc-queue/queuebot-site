QueueBot privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Who QueueBot is for

QueueBot is an internal tool for staff of Motory Group and First Union Financial II LLC. It is not offered to the public.

What we get from Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, QueueBot receives your name and email address. Nothing else from your Google account: not your contacts, your files, your email or your calendar.

What we use it for

  • Access: to check that you are on QueueBot's list of staff and to show you the screens your role needs.
  • Access log: to record when you sign in and what you do in QueueBot, so we can keep the work secure and fix problems.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising or share it outside Motory Group and First Union Financial II LLC.

Where it is kept

Your name, email, roles and the access log are kept in the company's Google Workspace, where only QueueBot's administrators can see them. Your sign-in lasts a few hours on your device and then asks again.

Questions or changes

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your access removed, contact eldinc@motorygroup.com.
